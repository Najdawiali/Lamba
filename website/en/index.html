Lamba is a local shopping app for Jordan. Search for a product or service, and see which shops near you have it before you head out.

For customers

  • Search for any product or service, in Arabic, in English, or even in everyday dialect.
  • See the nearby shops that have it, with the price and the location on a map.
  • Message the shop directly before you leave home.

For shops

  • Add your products by scanning barcodes, from a ready catalog of more than 10,000 products.
  • Let people in your area know what you have and how much it costs.
  • Answer customers' questions from the messages in the app.
